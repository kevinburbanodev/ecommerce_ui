# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Small-business owners in Colombia deciding whether to commission an online store. They open the demo from a services page, usually on a phone.
- Inside the fiction: people buying Colombian coffee to brew at home.

## Product Purpose

A demo online store selling single-origin Colombian coffee. It replaces the FakeStore catalogue (clothes, jewellery and hard drives in English and US dollars). The buyer picks a coffee by origin, roast and grind, adds it to the cart and completes a simulated checkout.

Success: an owner of a product business believes their own catalogue could look and sell like this.

## Positioning

A short catalogue where every coffee is told apart by where it comes from and how it tastes, and the choice of grind is part of the purchase.

## Operating Context

Spanish first, prices in Colombian pesos, mobile first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack stays: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, React Hook Form, Zod. No backend.
- Routes and behaviour to keep: catalogue, product detail, cart persisted in localStorage, validated checkout that is UI only and never charges anyone.
- The catalogue is local data; the FakeStore API is removed.
- Same repository and deployment.
- Photos come from free-licence libraries (Unsplash or Pexels). The list is approved by the owner before any download.
- Store name: Café Remite (invented).

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no real farms, growers, reviews, ratings, awards or cupping scores, and none may be invented as fact. Real coffee-growing regions may be named; the products, prices and tasting notes are illustrative and labelled as a demo.

## Product Principles

- Choosing a coffee should be easy for someone who does not know coffee.
- Every product is visibly different from the next one.
- Fiction is always labelled as a demo.
