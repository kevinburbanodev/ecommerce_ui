# Café Remite store (mode: persuade)

Audience: people buying coffee to brew at home, not experts. Task: choose a coffee and grind, put it in the envelope, finish a simulated order.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a coffee shop as a stamp issue: every coffee is its own stamp, the cart is the envelope, the order is the postmark. Refuses kraft bags, minimal black and equal product cards.
OWN-WORLD: airmail-blue paper, navy ink, perforated stamps printed in two or three saturated inks with engraved line landscapes; expanded sans legends; denominations as prices.
STORY: understands it sells single-origin Colombian coffee, picks by taste, believes ordering is simple, sends the order.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the issue of six stamps as a sheet across the screen, largest element on the page, with the taste filter and the envelope in the corner; one line that says what is sold.
FORM: postage stamps, candidate 5 of 7, seed fda8b00d. Signature: adding sticks the stamp on the envelope; checkout fields write the address on it; confirmation cancels it with a postmark.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
